Eco-Friendly Tips

🌱 Reduce Plastic Usage

Switch to reusable bags, bottles, and containers. Avoid single-use plastics.

💧 Save Water

Fix leaks, use low-flow showerheads, and turn off the tap while brushing teeth.

🌍 Conserve Energy

Turn off lights when not in use and switch to energy-efficient bulbs.

♻️ Recycle & Reuse

Sort your waste and try to repurpose items before discarding them.

🚲 Go Green

Opt for walking, cycling, or public transportation whenever possible.

🌳 Plant Trees

Participate in tree planting initiatives and grow indoor plants to improve air quality.

🍃 Support Sustainable Brands

Purchase from companies with eco-friendly practices and ethical standards.

🧺 Eco-Friendly Cleaning

Use natural cleaning products that are safe for the environment and reduce chemical pollution.
